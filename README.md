### 🔬 About Me

I am a 3rd-year **Computer Science & Engineering** undergraduate at **Jadavpur University** (CGPA: **8.68** | **WBJEE AIR 86** / 150,000+ candidates) and an undergraduate student researcher at **CMATER Lab** under **Prof. Ram Sarkar**.

My work bridges **mathematical rigor** (fuzzy evidence fusion, Poincaré hyperbolic embeddings, Minkowski functionals, and relativistic differential operators) with **practical systems engineering** (scalable agentic AI architectures, dual-stream geometric deep learning, and low-level C graphics & simulations).

- 🔭 **Researching**: Evidential deep learning (Sugeno & Choquet fuzzy integrals, Dempster-Shafer theory), physics-informed neural networks (PINNs for Dirac & relativistic equations), few-shot learning, and instance segmentation for histopathology.
- 🛠️ **Engineering**: High-performance AI agents (RAG pipelines, dual-memory vector + graph architectures, sub-second latency), and low-level computer graphics engines (ray tracing, fractal rendering in C).
- 🏆 **Milestones**: **Gold Medalist** in the **OpenAIMer 2026** ML Hackathon; Leader at JU Machine Learning Club; **500+ algorithmic problems solved** across LeetCode & Codeforces.
- 💬 **Ask me about**: Medical image segmentation, audio signal processing, C/C++ graphics from scratch, and AI Agent architecture.
